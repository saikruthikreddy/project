Based meticulously on the `titleToRefine` and driven primarily by the `userRefinementInstructions`, generate 2-3 distinct refined slide title suggestions that are NOT present in `alreadySuggestedRefinements`. REFINEMENT LOGIC:
1.  **Analyze `titleToRefine`:** Deeply understand its current length, structure, style, keywords, and the core message it attempts to convey.
2.  **Deconstruct `userRefinementInstructions`:** Identify the explicit changes, focus shifts, or stylistic alterations the user is seeking for `titleToRefine`.
3.  **Learn from Contextual Titles (Style & Length Guidance):**
    *   Closely examine `previousSlidesTitle` (if provided). Note its approximate length, sentence structure, tone, and level of detail.
    *   The refined suggestions should, by default, aim to achieve a style and level of detail consistent with `previousSlidesTitle` and the original `titleToRefine`, UNLESS `userRefinementInstructions` explicitly demand a different approach (e.g., "make this much shorter than previous titles," "adopt a more formal tone").
4.  **Apply Instructions Directly to `titleToRefine`:** This is your primary task.

    *   If instructed to change focus (e.g., "emphasize X"), re-angle `titleToRefine` to highlight that aspect, ensuring it's supported by `currentSlideContent`.
    *   If instructed to change format (e.g., to a question), transform `titleToRefine` accordingly.
5.  **Leverage `currentSlideContent` for Grounding & Substance:** If `userRefinementInstructions` ask for additions or emphasis (e.g., "add a key metric," "highlight the solution part"), source these details from `currentSlideContent` to enrich the refined title. 6.  **Align with `currentProjectPurpose`:** Ensure the refined title, especially its strategic angle or emphasis, aligns with the overarching goals of the project.
7.  **Use `consultingCraftPrinciplesForTitles` as Enhancement Tools:** After applying user instructions, thoughtfully use these principles to further polish the suggestions for clarity, impact, and professionalism, always respecting the primary guidance from `userRefinementInstructions` and the learned style from context.
8.  **Maintain Core Intent (Default):** Unless the `userRefinementInstructions` clearly indicate a desire to change the fundamental topic of the title, strive to preserve the core subject matter of `titleToRefine`. The goal is improvement and refinement of the *existing* title's message.
9.  **Offer Diverse Yet Relevant Refinements:** The 2-3 suggestions should ideally offer slightly different ways of achieving the user's requested improvements, all while being plausible refinements of the original `titleToRefine`. They must be distinct from `alreadySuggestedRefinements`.
GENERAL OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS:
*   Output 2-3 distinct refined suggestions.
